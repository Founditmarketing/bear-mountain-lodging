Only minutes from Beavers Bend State Park and Broken Bow Lake which is in the heart of Hochatown, Oklahoma, Fleur de Lake is a modern farmhouse-style cabin with a Mediterranean touch. Other nearby attractions include Mt Fork Brewery, Shuck Me Restaurant, Okie Girls Coffee & Ice Cream, and the Tasting Room. It is great for accommodating different size groups including couples, families, and friends. Another special amenity is the circle drive which is perfect for those who bring their boat or drive separate cars.

With an open concept floor plan on the main floor, you can enjoy the beautiful floor-to-ceiling gas fireplace as well as the large TV mounted above it from the living room and kitchen area. Also found in the living room is a couch with two chairs for your comfort. The dining area and bar in the kitchen is perfect for enjoying delicious meals or playing board games. The kitchen is fully stocked with all the cooking essentials needed for preparing meals if you prefer to dine in during your stay. For convenience, there is a half bath located downstairs as well. 

Downstairs, you will also find the king master suite including the ensuite bathroom that offers a large walk-in tile shower with natural light and a single sink vanity. For convenience, guests may access the beautiful back porch from this room to enjoy the natural setting. 

Up the spiral iron staircase, you will find the loft which has a daybed with trundle underneath, a shelf with board games for adults and kids, and TV for your entertainment. To your right is the 2nd king bedroom with 3 beautiful windows that allow natural light to shine through with your own personal mounted tv. Then to the left of the loft is a separate full-size bathroom with a walk-in tile shower and single vanity sink.

The outside amenities are on point. The back porch is enticing due to the canopy of trees that line the back of the property which makes you feel like you are in the middle of nowhere. You can enjoy all the nature around you while relaxing in the hot tub, or sitting around the table next to the gas fireplace. If you prefer other entertainment there is a large TV to watch sporting events or movies as well as corn hole boards if you are up for some friendly competition. Around the side of the house, you will discover a fire pit with lounge chairs surrounding it. There you can sit making memories, telling stories and  roasting marshmallows. There is also a front porch and sitting area where you can relax while gliding back and forth in the two rocking chairs or lounge on the sectional sofa. 

Fleur de Lake is the right choice if you are wanting to have luxury at your fingertips while wanting to experience nature at its best. 

This cabin is managed by Bear Mountain Management.

FINAL PAYMENT:  The balance is due 30 days prior to your arrival.
