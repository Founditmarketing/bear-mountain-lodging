Welcome to Glamping at the Bomasion!

Escape to Glamping at the Bomasion, a charming cabin nestled in scenic Broken Bow, Oklahoma, where exploring meets relaxation. This stunning modern luxury cabin is situated in the desirable West Fork Addition, just minutes from Beaver Bend State Park. Designed for family retreats or an adventure with friends seeking a memorable getaway, this spacious retreat promises to be the ideal sanctuary to unwind and have fun in nature. 

As you step inside, make your way to the inviting open-concept living space, where warm tones and soft furnishings create an atmosphere of home. The flickering flames of the gas fireplace cast a golden glow, inviting you to curl up on one of the couches after a day of exploration. Large windows frame views of the surrounding forest, bringing the beauty of nature indoors. 

The heart of any cabin, the fully equipped kitchen, is ready to bring out your inner chef. Marvel at the granite countertops and stainless steel appliances as you whip up a delicious breakfast or prepare a feast after spending your day exploring. The central island provides ample space for meal prep, allowing you to gather together while you cook. Enjoy sharing stories and laughter as you create culinary delights. The dining table is just a few steps away, so it's perfect for enjoying your meals and making lasting memories.

When it’s time to unwind, retreat to one of the beautifully appointed bedrooms. The master king bedroom with an ensuite, located on the main floor, beckons with its plush bed adorned with soft linens and fluffy pillows, ensuring restful nights. You can also step out onto the back deck to breathe in the fresh morning air or take in the stars before drifting off to sleep. With two additional cozy queen bedrooms upstairs, each thoughtfully designed with comfort in mind, with ensuite bathrooms, there’s plenty of space for everyone to recharge for tomorrow’s adventures.

The game room/loft adds an element of excitement to your stay, featuring a TV, arcade-style Pac-Man machine, lounge chairs, and a sleeper sofa for flexibility. You can also enjoy access to the upstairs balcony, where you can soak up the tranquility of the surroundings. 

Step outside to discover your very own outdoor oasis. The back deck welcomes you with comfy seating, including an outdoor dining table and TV, perfect for catching up on your favorite shows or sporting events, while enjoying the fresh air. It also features a grill, ideal for firing up delicious barbecues.

The fenced yard provides a wonderful space for pets and children to play, while the play set—complete with swings, a fort, and a slide—ensures hours of fun for the little ones. As the sun sets, adults can unwind in the swing near the fire pit, creating a cozy spot to relax and enjoy the warmth.

Be sure to gather around the fire pit for some quality time together—roasting marshmallows and making s'mores, where laughter and stories come alive beneath a starlit sky that will create memories that will last a lifetime. 

At the end of a long day, retreat to the covered porch and indulge in the hot tub, which is a perfect way to decompress. 

For those bringing their outdoor gear, the attached garage provides ample storage space, keeping everything organized and easily accessible as you embark on your hiking, fishing, or boating adventures at nearby Beavers Bend State Park. After a day filled with excitement, return to the cabin to relax, rejuvenate, and relish the simple pleasure of togetherness.

At Glamping at the Bomasion, every detail is designed to help you relax and reconnect with the people who matter most. Whether you’re seeking adventure or tranquility, you’ll find it all right here. Come create unforgettable memories in this serene haven, where every moment is an opportunity to enjoy the beauty of life with your loved ones.

CHECK-IN:  Check-in begins at 4:00 PM. If you plan to arrive after 4:00 PM, please email us at bearmountainlodging@gmail.com.  This will help us better prioritize our cleaning schedule during the busy times of year. Glamping at the Bomansion is a self-check-in cabin, and you will receive your directions via email 7 days before your arrival.

CHECK-OUT:  Check-out is at 11:00 AM.  To make departure day more efficient for everyone, please text  580-212-4746 if you decide to depart early.  This will help us with getting the next guest in on time.  The owner is not responsible for items left in the cabin.  If requested, items will be shipped for a $25.00 handling fee plus actual shipping costs.

Renter agrees to maintain the cleanliness of the cabin during their stay and leave the cabin in a reasonably clean condition when they depart.  All dirty dishes should be placed in the dishwasher and turned on before leaving the cabin.  The refrigerator needs to be emptied of all food. Tie up trash bags and place them in outside containers.  Please start a load of towels before your departure.  Please do not strip the beds. Our cleaning staff will take care of that and all the rest. Excessive cleaning fees could apply if the policy is not followed. 

DOWN PAYMENT:  We accept Visa, Mastercard, and Discover.  You will need to use one of the major credit cards to make a reservation.  The down payment (Reservation Fee) is 20% of the cost of the total reservation.  Down payments are applied toward the balance.  The down payment (Reservation Fee) is nonrefundable. 

FINAL PAYMENT:  The balance is due 30 days prior to your arrival.

AGE REQUIREMENT: You must be 25 years of age to reserve this vacation rental.  This cabin is limited to families or adults 25 years of age or older. For couples (2 Adults) the age limit is 21 years of age. We reserve the right to refuse service with monies forfeited if any discrepancies in our policies are suspected. 

MAXIMUM OCCUPANCY-8 people:  Do not exceed the maximum occupancy allowed for the cabin.  Guests who violate this restriction will be asked to leave and will lose all rental fees.  We do require a list of names and ages of guests before your arrival if you did not provide it at the time of your reservation.

RESERVATION REQUIREMENT: There is a 2-night minimum required.  There is a 3 or 4-night minimum for holidays and high peak season. 

CHANGING CABINS OR DATES:  Date changes or removing nights are subject to a $50 fee. You cannot make a change if you are within 30 days of your arrival date. Reservations cannot be moved from one cabin to another.

NO HOUSE PARTIES OR EVENTS:  House parties or events are strictly prohibited.  Rental guests understand that we will accept families, married couples, and responsible adults 25 years of age and older ONLY.  Renters acknowledge that violation of this agreement will lead to their being required to leave with NO refund.  Guests must state, in advance, the number of renters/guests during their stay at Glamping at the Bomansion. 

ATV RIDING IS PROHIBITED ON THE PREMISES:  It is illegal to ride ATVs on the area roads if you are not properly tagged. 

PETS ARE ALLOWED: This cabin is pet friendly and allows up to 2 dogs, above the age of one, that are 40 pounds or less each, with a $75.00 NON-REFUNDABLE FEE per pet. You will be required to provide the name of your pet, the breed, age, and weight.

NO DAILY HOUSEKEEPING SERVICE IS PROVIDED.

NO SMOKING OF ANY KIND IN CABIN:  We ask that you do not smoke inside the cabin. This also includes no vaping, no e-cigarettes, and no marijuana.  If you do smoke outside, please keep the doors closed, so it does not go into the cabin.  We advertise the cabin as non-smoking, and it is difficult to get the odor out of the cabin for the next guests. Ashtrays will be provided outside.  Please put your cigarette butts in the ashtrays.  Non-compliance will result in a $100 per day charge. 

SHOOTING GUNS OR FIREWORKS is prohibited on cabin property. 

HOT TUBS & COVERS:  Our hot tubs are cleaned and chemically treated after every rental.  Hot tub use is strictly at your own risk.  Persons with high blood pressure, skin sensitivities, or who might be pregnant are strongly urged not to use a hot tub. 

PLEASE do not allow anyone in your group to walk on, jump on, or play on the hot tub covers, they will break.  The condition of the hot tub is checked after every checkout and before your arrival.

WE LOVE OUR TREES!  Please do not cut on or cut down trees on the property.  Fines will be charged to your account for damaging trees. 

PROPERTY ELECTRONIC EQUIPMENT: Please do not tamper with the electronics, television, receivers, etc., equipment in the cabin.  Also, please do not unhook or disconnect any cords or wires from any of the equipment.  We do check the TVs, DirecTV receivers, and surround sound to ensure they are in working order. Please make sure everything is in working order before you leave.   A $100 fee will be charged for unhooking or tampering with any electronic device.

MAINTENANCE PROBLEMS will be addressed in as timely a manner as possible. However, the rental rate cannot be adjusted due to any malfunction of equipment (which includes  T.V., VCR, DVD, heating units, hot water heaters, stoves, refrigerators, hot tubs, locks, etc). In the extremely rare case where there is a major problem with heat, hot water, etc, that makes the home uninhabitable, we will try to arrange a different accommodation.  If that is not possible, a prorated amount will be refunded.
