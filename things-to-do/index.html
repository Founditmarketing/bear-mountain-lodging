ATV Maps & Information
Printable ATV Map

Take adventuring to the next level. The Ouachita National Forest contains hundreds of miles of designated off-highway vehicle routes so you can cover more ground. Discover the wildlife and wonders of the deep forest and maybe get a little muddy in the process. Be sure to contact the U.S. Forest Service for rules and regulations regarding allowed vehicles, motor vehicle use maps and seasonal changes.

Want to bring your own atvs to ride during your stay? 

Directions To ATV Trails:

From Hochatown, you will go north on HWY 259 about 6-7 miles. (This is the main road that goes through Hochatown.) You’ll then come to a road (on the left-hand side) and will see a small sign that says 53,000 on it. Take a left on Road 53000. There will be a big circle area where you can park, and then ride your ATVs. Be sure to pay close attention to any signs posted because there are some that say you can’t ride in certain areas, but they will be labeled. 

Download the Avenza Offline Maps App!

Don’t let spotty cell phone service or data connection issues let you down.  Downloading the Avenza Offline Maps App is the best way to navigate while riding the trails at Broken Bow Lake!

Beaver’s Bend State Park is one of Oklahoma’s most popular parks, and rightly so! As a whole, the park is a vast 1,300 miles and hosts a wide variety of activities sure to satisfy any and all outdoor lovers. You’ll find opportunities for hiking, biking, fishing, tennis, volleyball, golf and mini-golf, swimming, paddle boarding, canoeing and kayaking, horseback riding, nature center activities, picnicking and more! 

And don’t forget the spectacular scenery! Beaver’s Bend State Park is nestled in the Kiamichi Mountains along the shores of Broken Bow Lake and Mountain Fork River passes through it. Get your fill of sunshine, wildlife, and jaw-dropping views.

During your stay at a Bear Mountain Lodging cabin, we hope you’ll take the time to visit the lovely Broken Bow Lake. This is one of the most gorgeous lakes in Oklahoma. Broken Bow Lake covers 14,000 acres and has a vast 180 miles of scenic shoreline. Enjoy stunning views from rock cliffs. Pack a picnic and watch the birds. Adventure through the heavily wooded surrounding land. Go for a swim in the clear, deep water. Those who love the outdoors will not be disappointed. 

Water lovers will find plenty to do. Bring your own boat or rent one from Beavers Bend Marina. The Marina offers speed boats, pontoons, houseboats, and jet skis. For a slower pace, enjoy canoeing or kayaking. Broken Bow Lake is also a popular attraction for fishing as it houses many types of fish including bass, catfish, crappie, sunfish, and walleye! Local fishing guides are available. Whatever your outdoor fancy may be, a visit to Broken Bow Lake is sure to be memorable.

Spend an unforgettable day at Mountain Fork River. The river runs through Beavers Bend Resort Park and hosts rapids, small waterfalls, and breathtaking views of the Ouachita Mountains. Majestic Bald Cypress trees line much of the shoreline. Adventurers will be pleased to hear that the river is renowned as the best white water stream in Oklahoma! For a more peaceful pace, try canoeing or kayaking instead. The river offers year round trout fishing for both Rainbow and Brown trout. Fly fishing is also very popular. Add a visit to Mountain Fork River to your Oklahoma bucket list!
