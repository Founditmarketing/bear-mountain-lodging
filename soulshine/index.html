Soulshine, formerly known as Wildcat Den, is a Bohemian inspired farmhouse accented with cheerful, cozy and colorful decor.  The cabin is nestled in the serene Eagle Mountain area off paved roads, just a stone's throw away from a plethora of delightful dining options and exciting activities including Mountain Fork Brewery, Shuck Me Bar and Grill, The Tasting Room, Okie Girls Ice Cream, and Hochatown Escape Games. The cabin offers convenience and adventure in equal measure  with the picturesque Broken Bow Lake and Beavers Bend State Park within close reach. Nature lovers are in for a treat!

As you step into the living room, your gaze is drawn to the panoramic back wall of floor-to-ceiling windows, framing a breathtaking view of the lush backyard oasis. Daylight floods the space, illuminating the tasteful decor and inviting ambiance. Whether you're gathering around the cozy fireplace, savoring a home-cooked meal at the dining table, or lounging on the plush sofa, every moment spent here is infused with warmth and elegance.

The open-concept layout seamlessly connects the gourmet kitchen and inviting living room, creating an inviting space for mingling and meal preparation. Filled with modern amenities and sleek finishes, the kitchen is a chef's dream, equipped with everything needed to create culinary delights.

This retreat is designed for comfort and enjoyment for a group of friends or a family. With three king bedrooms and three bathrooms, there’s plenty of space for everyone to relax independently or together. The master suite features a plush king bed and a private ensuite bathroom, offering a perfect space for seeking relaxation. Two additional king bedrooms ensure that everyone has their own comfortable space, with easy access to nearby full bathrooms. Upstairs, a cheerful loft hosts two queen beds, making it ideal for children or extra guests. Every king bedroom is furnished with a TV, spacious closet, and dresser, providing ample storage and entertainment options for your convenience.

The outdoor backyard is a true oasis. It is rare to find such a large spacious private yard. With a charming seasonal creek meandering through the property, it adds a touch of tranquility to the surroundings. For the little ones, there's a playground and a metal climbing dome to keep them entertained for hours. Guests inclined towards friendly competition can enjoy the horseshoe pit and corn hole boards. The centrally located fire pit beckons everyone to gather around, share stories, toast marshmallows, and create lasting memories.

Venture to the lower deck to unwind in the inviting hot tub, adorned with twinkling string lights for a magical ambiance. The expansive top deck provides the perfect setting to witness breathtaking moments, whether you're firing up the grill or enjoying a quiet moment with a cup of coffee at dawn. The backyard vista is truly spectacular, beckoning all to savor the beauty and create unforgettable experiences.

This is more than just a cabin—it's a sanctuary where couples, families or a group of friends can cherish memories that are made and shared. 

This cabin is managed by Bear Mountain Management.

CHECK-IN: Check-in begins at 4:00 PM. If you plan to arrive after 4:00 PM, please email us at bearmountainlodging@gmail.com.  This will help us better prioritize our cleaning schedule during the busy times of year. Soulshine is a self check-in cabin, and you will receive your directions via email 7 days before your arrival.

CHECK-OUT: Check-out is at 11:00 AM.  To make departure day more efficient for everyone, please text  580-212-4746 if you decide to depart early.  This will help us with getting the next guest in on time.  Owner is not responsible for items left in the cabin.  If requested, items will be shipped for a $25.00 handling fee plus actual shipping costs.

Renter agrees to maintain cleanliness of the cabin during their stay and leave the cabin in a reasonably clean condition when they depart.  All dirty dishes should be placed in the dishwasher and turned on before leaving the cabin.  Refrigerator needs to be emptied of all food.  Tie up trash bags and place them in outside containers.  Please start a load of towels before your departure.  Please do not strip the beds. Our cleaning staff will take care of that and all the rest. Excessive cleaning fees could apply if policy is not followed.

DOWN PAYMENT: We accept Visa, Mastercard, and Discover.  You will need to use one of the major credit cards to make a reservation.  The down payment (Reservation Fee) is 20% of the cost of the total reservation.  Down payments are applied toward the balance.  The down payment (Reservation Fee) is nonrefundable.

FINAL PAYMENT: The balance is due 30 days prior to your arrival.

AGE REQUIREMENT: You must be 25 years of age to reserve this vacation rental.  This cabin is limited to families or adults 25 years of age or older. We reserve the right to refuse service with monies forfeited if any discrepancies in our policies are suspected.  

MAXIMUM OCCUPANCY-10 people: Do not exceed the maximum occupancy allowed for the cabin.  Guests that violate this restriction will be asked to leave and will lose all rental fees.  We do require a list of names and ages of guests before your arrival if you did not provide it at the time of your reservation.

RESERVATION REQUIREMENT: There is a 2 night minimum required.  There is a 3 or 4 night minimum for holidays and high peak season.

CHANGING CABINS OR DATES: Date changes or removing nights are subject to a $50 fee. You cannot make a change if you are within 30 days of your arrival date. Reservations cannot be moved from one cabin to another.

NO HOUSE PARTIES OR EVENTS: House parties or events are strictly prohibited.  Rental guests understand that we will accept families, married couples, and responsible adults 25 years of age and older ONLY.  Renters acknowledge that violation of this agreement will lead to your being required to leave with NO refund.  Guests must state, in advance, the number of renters/guests during their stay at High Interest.  

ATV RIDING IS PROHIBITED ON THE PREMISES: It is illegal to ride ATVs on the area roads also if you are not properly tagged.

PETS NOT ALLOWED:

We know you love your fur babies, and we do too. However, we do not allow pets at Soulshine.  Non-Compliance of our NO PET policy will result in a $100 per day charge. Again, guests that violate this restriction will be asked to leave and will lose all rental fees.

NO DAILY HOUSEKEEPING SERVICE IS PROVIDED.

NO SMOKING OF ANY KIND IN CABIN: We ask that you do not smoke inside the cabin. This also includes no vaping, no e-cigarettes, and no marijuana.  If you do smoke outside, please keep the doors closed, so it does not go into the cabin.  We advertise the cabins as no smoking, and it is difficult to get the odor out of the cabin for the next guests. Ashtrays will be provided outside.  Please put your cigarette butts in the ashtrays.  Non-compliance will result in a $100 per day charge. 

SHOOTING GUNS OR FIREWORKS is prohibited on cabin property. 

HOT TUBS & COVERS: Our hot tubs are cleaned and chemically treated after every rental.  Hot tub use is strictly at your own risk.  Persons with high blood pressure, skin sensitivities, or who might be pregnant are strongly urged not to use a hot tub.

PLEASE do not allow anyone in your group to walk-on, jump-on or play-on the hot tub covers, they will break.  The condition of the hot tub is checked after every checkout and before your arrival.

WE LOVE OUR TREES! Please do not cut on or cut down trees on the property.  Fines will be charged to your account for damaging trees.

PROPERTY ELECTRONIC EQUIPMENT: Please do not tamper with the electronics, television, receivers, etc. equipment in the cabin.  Also please do not unhook or disconnect any cords or wires from any of the equipment.  We do check the TV’s, DirecTv receivers, and surround sound to ensure they are in working order. Please make sure everything is in working order before you leave.   A $100 fee will be charged for unhooking or tampering with any electronic device.

MAINTENANCE PROBLEMS will be addressed in as timely a manner as possible. However, the rental rate cannot be adjusted due to any malfunction of equipment (which includes  T.V., VCR, DVD, heating units, hot water heaters, stoves, refrigerators, hot tubs, locks etc). In the extremely rare case where there is a major problem with heat, hot water, etc that makes the home uninhabitable, we will try to arrange a different accommodation.  If that is not possible, a prorated amount will be refunded.

If anyone neglects to follow the rules, policies, and suggestions of Bear Mountain Management, LLC the guests cannot hold us responsible for their actions, accidents, or neglect.  Once your first payment is received, it is considered a rental agreement and is considered a legal and binding contract.
